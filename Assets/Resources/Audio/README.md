# KICK Back original audio

All WAV files are composed and synthesized from oscillators and seeded, filtered noise by `Tools/generate_audio.py`. No external samples, commercial recordings, copied melodies, vocals, or third-party music are included. They can be used and modified with this project without third-party attribution.

32 kHz / 16-bit PCM. Music is stereo; combat effects are mono. Lobby: 90 BPM / eight bars. Battle: 116 BPM / eight bars. Dojaehak boss: 132 BPM / sixteen bars with sparse minor motifs, a low pulse and tom fills. Wrapped tails and short seam fades make the masters loop safely.

Punch, kick, heavy, ultimate, guard, parry, dodge and defeat use separate original effects. Music is mastered to comparable RMS levels; music and effect gain are independently configurable. Browser playback requires a user gesture. The mixer limits effect voices, gives parries/finishers priority, ducks music briefly on confirmed impact, and pauses music/effects immediately with Esc.
